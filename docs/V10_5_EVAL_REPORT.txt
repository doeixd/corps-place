WHAT v10.5 IS
  v10.4 (field-pace + tau0.7/hew2.0 spread-loss) nearly halved global under-prediction
  (V10 -0.88 -> v10.4 -0.49) but its RESIDUAL BIAS IS DIVISION-DEPENDENT: on the honest
  07-12..07-16 holdout, World Class still under-predicts (-0.69) while Open Class is already
  zeroed (+0.00). A single global tau that zeroes WC pushes OC positive -> loss tuning alone
  cannot fix both (V10_5_NOTES sec.2c). v10.5 adds a THIN, REVERSIBLE, per-(division x
  percent-through bucket) OFFSET on v10.4's pooled 8-seed ensemble. NO retrain.

LEAKAGE DISCIPLINE (the #1 risk here -- documented exactly)
  For each target show at date d, the offset for (division D, pct-bucket b) is
      offset(D,b) = shrink * robust_mean( actual - pred )  over resolved rows with
                    division D, bucket b, competition_date STRICTLY < d, within a 14-day
                    recency window;   shrink = n/(n+8)  (thin bucket -> ~no-op).
  The fit NEVER sees the target row, any same-day row, or any later show. Verified by the
  strictly-< guard in Recal.fit_offset() and by the per-target fit-count audit below (every
  offset is fit only on earlier-dated rows). residual is trimmed (drop min/max) and |offset|
  capped at 1.5 so a cold-start blowup cannot move the layer. Reversible: off == plain v10.4
  (the [consistency] line proves the un-recalibrated ensemble reproduces v10.4 exactly).

  Fit-pool note (disclosed): the pre-07-12 2026 shows were IN v10.4's training window
  (trained on 2026<=07-11), so their residuals are partly in-sample and may UNDERSTATE the
  true out-of-sample bias. MSE range-compression is an underfitting effect visible in-sample,
  so the division split still shows through; the recency window keeps the fit on the most
  relevant recent shows. This in-sample optimism is why the leakage-safe layer corrects the
  PREDICTABLE part of the WC bias but cannot fully zero the finals-week growth (see VERDICT).

VERDICT
================================================================================
DID the leakage-safe division recal zero BOTH divisions' bias, without overshoot, ranking
loss, or majority regression?  ->  PARTIAL YES on WC, YES on the guardrails.

  * World Class bias   -0.689 -> -0.491   (|bias| -0.184, show-bootstrap P(better)=0.96):
      corrects ~29% of the WC gap. NOT fully zeroed -- see caveat.
  * Open Class bias    +0.001 -> -0.008   (unchanged; P=0.44 == coin flip): held AT ~0,
      NOT pushed positive, NOT overshot. The division-aware layer leaves the already-calibrated
      division essentially untouched -- exactly the point of making it per-division.
  * No overshoot: neither division crosses to a positive bias.
  * Ranking HELD EXACTLY: per-show Spearman 0.990079 -> 0.990079 (delta 0.000000), even though
      4/6 holdout shows are mixed-division (the per-division differential offset is small enough
      that no within-show ordering flips).
  * Global bias -0.494 -> -0.355 (closer to 0 than v10.4, as required).
  * de-biased MAE 0.811 -> 0.760 and total MAE 0.843 -> 0.777 both IMPROVE; the data-rich mid
      tercile improves (-0.354 -> -0.180) and bottom holds (-0.567 -> -0.535). No regression.

WHY WC ISN'T FULLY ZEROED (the honest, important finding)
  The WC under-prediction GROWS into finals week: on the holdout its residual is -0.16 on 07-13,
  +0.72 on 07-14, +1.45 on 07-16 (latest show). A LEAKAGE-SAFE recal can only learn the part of
  the bias that PRIOR shows reveal (~+0.2 WC under-prediction), so it applies +0.19..+0.28 to WC
  and correctly cannot anticipate the +1.45 finals jump from earlier data -- that would require
  seeing the future. This is precisely the "championship compression = finals-week EXTRAPOLATION
  artifact" the v10.4 report and V10_5_NOTES sec.2c identified: the structural fix is the AUGUST
  full-season retrain (more top-of-range training targets), NOT more recal and NOT higher global
  tau. The recal is the right thin closer for the PREDICTABLE division-level drift; it delivers
  its full available signal here without overshooting or breaking ranking.

RECOMMENDATION
  SHIP the recal as a thin, reversible serving layer (division x pct-bucket, expanding window,
  14-day recency, shrink n/(n+8), trimmed, |offset|<=1.5). It strictly improves bias and MAE on
  the holdout with zero ranking cost and zero OC overshoot. Re-fit it (and retrain v10.4's loss
  on full-season data) once August finals land -- that is what closes the residual WC finals gap.
